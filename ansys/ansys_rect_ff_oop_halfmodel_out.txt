     2  !  ansys_rect_ff_oop_halfmodel.inp  --  Mechanical APDL batch deck  
     3  !  FIRST FE CROSS-CHECK for the rectangular free-free (FFFF) OOP candidates 
     4  !  found by probe_rect_freefree_oop_discovery.py (job 2322815) and confirmed
     5  !  to deepen under basis enlargement by probe_rect_freefree_oop_basisbump.py
     6  !  (job 2322845). EXPLORATORY -- first FE pass, NOT yet a validated gate the
     7  !  way the annular FF-P1 tables are. No SOLVER_VERSION implications.
     8  !   
     9  !  WHY A HALF-MODEL: the solver's SYM/ANTI classes are parity in x2 (the
    10  !  breadth direction) about x2=0 -- sin(xi*x2+phin), phin=pi/2 (SYM, even)  
    11  !  or phin=0 (ANTI, odd). A half-model (Y in [0,B], mirror cut at Y=0) with 
    12  !  a SYMM or ANTI boundary condition on the cut isolates exactly one parity 
    13  !  class per pass, the flat-plate analogue of ansys_ff_oop_halfsector.inp's 
    14  !  bisector technique -- except here the mirror plane (global Y=0) is   
    15  !  already aligned with the natural Cartesian axes, so no local coordinate  
    16  !  system or NROTAT is needed: for a shell whose normal is global Z, mirror 
    17  !  symmetry about a Y=0 plane is the standard ANSYS symmetric/antisymmetric 
    18  !  DOF pattern taken directly in GLOBAL Cartesian coordinates:  
    19  !     SYMM (even in x2, matches solver phin=pi/2):  UY=0, ROTX=0, ROTZ=0
    20  !     ASYM (odd in x2,  matches solver phin=0):      UX=0, UZ=0,  ROTY=0
    21  !   
    22  !  GEOMETRY/MATERIAL (a chosen benchmark instance -- ANY consistent set of  
    23  !  b,h,E,nu,rho gives the SAME solver Lambda locations; this pins one   
    24  !  physical instance so Ansys can be run and compared in Hz):   
    25  !    half-breadth B=1.0 m, aspect ratio l/b=1.5 (lob, matching the solver   
    26  !    probes AND this project's own validated rect-cantilever l/b=1.5 case,  
    27  !    LESSONS_LEARNED Sec 11.2) so half-length L=1.5 m (full plate 3.0 x 2.0 
    28  !    m), full thickness H=0.04 m (H/(2B)=0.02, thin-plate valid, same order 
    29  !    as the annular FF-P1 benchmark's H/R_o~0.01), E=210 GPa, nu=0.30,  
    30  !    rho=7800 kg/m^3 (identical material constants to every other Ansys 
    31  !    deck in this project, for consistency).
    32  !   
    33  !  Lambda -> Hz CONVERSION (derived directly from Seok/Tiersten/Scarton 2004
    34  !  rect Part 1, Eqs. 24-28, 37-38, 41-42; independently re-derived and cross-   
    35  !  checked against this project's own T=1,R=1,mu=2T-nu=1.7 isotropic reduction  
    36  !  in RectOOPAssembler.__init__ -- see LESSONS_LEARNED item 16/Sec 11 for the   
    37  !  solver-side convention): 
    38  !    D_bar = (2*h_half^3/3)*c11_bar,  c11_bar=E/(1-nu^2)  (Eq.24, h_half =  
    39  !      HALF thickness = H/2 -- reduces to the standard D=E*H^3/(12(1-nu^2)) 
    40  !      once h_half=H/2 is substituted, confirmed algebraically) 
    41  !    isotropic reduction: R=1, T=1, mu_bar=2T-nu=1.7 (Eqs.26-28, matches
    42  !      RectOOPAssembler exactly)
    43  !    omega_bar = (pi/(2*B))*sqrt(c66/rho),  c66=G=E/(2(1+nu))      (Eq.38)  
    44  !    k_bar = (B/(pi*h_half))*sqrt(6*(T-nu)/R) = (B/(pi*h_half))*sqrt(6*(1-nu))  
    45  !      for isotropic T=R=1                                          (Eq.42) 
    46  !    Lambda = k_bar * Omega_bar  (what Table 3 / the solver's Lambda IS)
    47  !    => f[Hz] = Lambda * (pi*h_half)/(4*B^2) * sqrt(G/(6*rho*(1-nu)))   
    48  !    KFAC (below) IS this Hz-per-unit-Lambda constant, computed from the
    49  !    SAME E/nu/rho/H/B as the mesh, so LAMBDA_FE = FREQ/KFAC converts every 
    50  !    extracted modal frequency back into the solver's own Lambda units --   
    51  !    the comparison below happens in Lambda space, not Hz, so no rounding   
    52  !    of the conversion constant itself enters the reported error.   
    53  !   
    54  !  SOLVER-SIDE REFERENCE TABLE (job 2322845, corrected n_cpair basis-bump,  
    55  !  6 of 7 candidates confirmed deepening >4 orders of magnitude, both corner
    56  !  variants agreeing on Lambda* -- see LESSONS_LEARNED item 16):
    57  !    SYM   Lambda*=0.056   (variant A/B agree)  
    58  !    SYM   Lambda*=0.412   (variant A/B agree)  
    59  !    SYM   Lambda*=0.431   (variant A/B agree)  
    60  !    SYM   Lambda*=0.864   (variant A/B agree)  
    61  !    SYM   Lambda*=0.960-0.962 (variant A/B differ by 0.002, edge-of-window)
    62  !    ANTI  Lambda*=0.475   (variant A/B agree)  
    63  !  ANTI Lambda*~0.97 is EXCLUDED -- its baseline basis was pathologically   
    64  !  thin (nfull=1) and A/B disagreed on location even before the bump; not   
    65  !  yet trustworthy enough to benchmark. Do not read a "miss" on it as   
    66  !  informative either way.  
    67  !   
    68  !  MODE-FAMILY ASSUMPTION (simplifying vs. the annular deck, stated so it   
    69  !  can be checked): SHELL281 models both flexural and membrane behavior in  
    70  !  one element, but for this thin a plate (H/(2B)=0.02) in-plane extensional
    71  !  modes are expected roughly an order of magnitude or more above this  
    72  !  window (the annular case's IP modes were ~150-1100 Hz vs OOP ~15-103 Hz, 
    73  !  a similar ratio) -- so a plain frequency-window cutoff (<30 Hz) should   
    74  !  isolate flexural OOP modes without a separate PLANE183 run. If an
    75  !  unexpected extra mode appears inside the target window with no nearby
    76  !  solver candidate, do NOT assume it is spurious -- check its mode shape   
    77  !  (dominant UZ = flexural, dominant UX/UY = membrane) before discounting it.   
    78  !   
    79  !  PRE-REGISTERED READING (do not reinterpret after the fact):  
    80  !    0. CONVERGENCE GATE: mesh1-equiv vs mesh2-equiv elastic modes (same
    81  !       parity pass) must agree <0.3% before anything else is read. 
    82  !    1. Compare each of the 6 SYM/ANTI candidates ONLY against its OWN  
    83  !       parity pass's mode list (SYM candidates vs SYMM pass; the ANTI  
    84  !       candidate vs ASYM pass) -- a match in the WRONG parity pass does
    85  !       not count.  
    86  !    2. A match within ~1-2% in LAMBDA_FE units at the correct parity is
    87  !       the first genuine EXTERNAL evidence for that candidate (upgrading   
    88  !       it beyond internal-consistency-only). A candidate with no match in  
    89  !       its own parity pass within a wide window is evidence against it (or 
    90  !       could indicate this specific corner-term construction predicts the  
    91  !       wrong location even though it deepens with basis -- distinguish by  
    92  !       checking if the OTHER variant's Lambda* [not tested by this deck,   
    93  !       see the basisbump job log] would have matched better).  
    94  !    3. If ALL SIX candidates miss by roughly the SAME multiplicative   
    95  !       factor, suspect a KFAC/geometry conversion error, not a physics 
    96  !       problem -- recheck the Lambda-to-Hz derivation before concluding
    97  !       the candidates are wrong.   
    98  !    This is a FIRST FE PASS on ONE geometry instance -- exploratory, not a 
    99  !    validated gate. No SOLVER_VERSION action, no table promotion from this 
   100  !    alone regardless of outcome.
