     6  !  executed until now): the OOP ceiling item -- solver dip raw=2.828013 
     7  !  (Omega_lit ~= 111.65) vs the one unmatched FE mode Omega_lit = 111.4627

     9  !  bump, sensitivity map, null-vector gap; jobs 2316969/2317010/2317114)
    10  !  without resolution, and Sec I explicitly names ONE genuinely new lever:  
    11  !  classify the FE mode's PARITY about the sector bisector independently,   
    12  !  sidestepping the rM/gap circularity entirely. The free-free wave 
    13  !  solution's K is block-diagonal in parity (post-B12), so every solver 
    14  !  dip lives in a definite parity block. If the FE 111.4627 mode's parity   
    15  !  matches the block hosting the 2.828013 dip, that is independent  
    16  !  evidence FOR the match; if it is the OTHER parity, the documented
    17  !  model-vs-FE gap is confirmed structurally (the solver's candidate
    18  !  cannot be that FE mode at all).

    20  !  METHOD: model HALF the sector (0..45 deg; full sector is 0..90, bisector 
    21  !  at 45) with the identical SHELL281 setup as ansys_ff_oop_ext.inp, all
    22  !  outer edges free, and the cut edge at theta=45 given (pass S) SYMMETRY   
    23  !  or (pass A) ANTISYMMETRY conditions about the bisector r-z plane. In 
    24  !  cylindrical nodal coordinates (x=radial, y=hoop, z=axial):   
    25  !     SYMM: UY=0, ROTX=0, ROTZ=0   (DSYM,SYMM,Y,11) 
    26  !     ANTI: UX=0, UZ=0,  ROTY=0    (DSYM,ASYM,Y,11) 
    27  !  Each full-sector FFFF elastic mode appears in EXACTLY ONE of the two 
    28  !  passes at (mesh-convergence-level) the same frequency: SYMM pass = EVEN  
    29  !  parity, ANTI pass = ODD parity. Two mesh passes per BC for the   
    30  !  convergence gate (radial 64 / arc 32, then radial 96 / arc 48 -- 
    31  !  element sizes matched to the full-sector mesh64/mesh96 runs).
    32  !   
    33  !  GEOMETRY/MATERIAL (identical to ansys_ff_oop_ext.inp): R_i=4, R_o=8, 
    34  !  half-angle 45 deg, H=0.08, E=210e9, nu=0.30, rho=7800.   
    35  !  Omega_lit = w*R_o^2*sqrt(rho*H/D), D=E*H^3/(12(1-nu^2)). 
    36  !   
    37  !  MODE-FAMILY SEPARATION: as in ansys_ff_oop_ext.inp -- discard any mode   
    38  !  whose f[Hz] matches the PLANE183 pure-IP list (ip_modes_mesh80.txt, job  
    39  !  2316743) within ~0.5%; discard ~0 Hz rigid modes; the remainder are OOP.

    41  !  together should reproduce the full-sector inventory -- a free
    42  !  completeness check.)
