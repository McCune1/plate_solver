     7  !  published orthotropic free-free tables of Shi, Liang and Wang" 8/8 to

    11  !  (jve18517004.pdf, D. Shi/Q. Liang/Q. Wang/X. Teng, J. Vibroengineering   
    12  !  18(5) 2016, 3138-3152) this session found these 8 numbers do NOT appear  
    13  !  anywhere in it -- its own Table 2 FFFF row, same geometry (phi=90,   
    14  !  R_i/R_o=0.5) and same orthotropic material, gives 0.0046-0.0479, two to  
    15  !  three orders of magnitude off. This deck does NOT try to resolve that
    16  !  citation question by more paper archaeology -- it independently  
    17  !  regenerates the orthotropic FFFF spectrum from a fresh FE model so the   
    18  !  "Ansys ortho-FFFF truth" label can actually be checked against a live
    19  !  ANSYS run, not just trusted from a several-week-old note.
    20  !   
    21  !  GEOMETRY: identical physical plate used throughout this project's FF-P1  
    22  !  isotropic benchmark (R_i=4, R_o=8 m, PHI=90 deg sector, H=0.08 m full
    23  !  thickness) -- R_i/R_o=0.5 matches what both the paper and the disputed   
    24  !  target list claim.   
    25  !   
    26  !  MATERIAL (orthotropic, Shi/Liang/Wang's stated properties, matching  
    27  !  plate_solver.geometry.OrthotropicMaterial's constructor args E_r=40e9,   
    28  !  E_theta=70e9, nu_r=0.30, G_rtheta=3.51e9, rho=7850): 
    29  !    E_r     = 40 GPa   (radial Young's modulus, local element X after ESYS,11) 
    30  !    E_theta = 70 GPa   (tangential Young's modulus, local element Y)   
    31  !    G_rtheta= 3.51 GPa (in-plane shear -- drives the D66/torsional stiffness)  
    32  !    nu_r    = 0.30     (= ANSYS PRXY when X=r, Y=theta: eps_theta=-PRXY*eps_r  
    33  !                         under uniaxial sigma_r; ANSYS derives PRYX via its
    34  !                         own reciprocity, matching this project's own  
    35  !                         nu_theta = nu_r*E_theta/E_r = 0.525 convention -- 
    36  !                         see geometry.py's OrthotropicMaterial.__init__)   
    37  !    rho     = 7850 kg/m^3  
    38  !  Shi's paper gives ONLY these four in-plane/shear constants -- no 
    39  !  through-thickness (Z-direction) data. EZ/PRYZ/PRXZ/GYZ/GXZ below are 
    40  !  placeholders (EZ=E_theta, PRYZ=PRXZ=nu_r, GYZ=GXZ=G_rtheta) that do NOT  
    41  !  affect the result: SHELL281's shell-section stiffness is built from the  
    42  !  plane-stress-reduced EX/EY/PRXY/GXY block (sigma_Z=0 is enforced by the  
    43  !  shell formulation itself), and GYZ/GXZ only set the transverse-shear 
    44  !  stiffness, which is negligible at this plate's H/R_o=0.01 thinness   
    45  !  regardless of its exact value (same uniform-G simplification the 
    46  !  existing validated ansys_ff_oop_ext.inp/ansys_ff_isoval.inp isotropic
    47  !  decks already use for all three shear planes).   
    48  !   
    49  !  OMEGA_LIT CONVENTION: matches plate_solver.geometry._omega_lit exactly --
    50  !  Omega_lit = omega * R_o^2 * sqrt(rho*H/D), D = c11_bar*H^3/12, c11_bar = 
    51  !  E_r/(1 - nu_r*nu_theta) (the orthotropic generalization of E/(1-nu^2);   
    52  !  reduces to it when E_r=E_theta). Computed inline below as C11BAR/DD/KLIT.
    53  !   
    54  !  MODE-FAMILY IDENTIFICATION: a fully free SHELL281 model interleaves  
    55  !  rigid-body, out-of-plane (flexural), and in-plane (extensional) modes in 
    56  !  one spectrum, same as every other FFFF deck in this project. Rather than 
    57  !  needing a companion PLANE183 run to discard IP modes by frequency
    58  !  matching (as ansys_ff_oop_ext.inp does for the isotropic case), THIS deck
    59  !  computes an OOP-dominance energy fraction for every extracted mode --
    60  !  UzFrac = SUM(UZ^2) / SUM(UX^2+UY^2+UZ^2) -- the same *VOPER(MULT) +  
    61  !  *VSCFUN(SUM) idiom already proven working in ansys_ff_isoval_tagged.inp  
    62  !  (job 2313082) and ansys_ff_oop_mac_extract.inp (job 2323990's fix).  
    63  !  UzFrac near 1.0 = OOP-flexural mode (what we want); near 0 = in-plane
    64  !  mode; intermediate = near-degenerate mixing (flag for manual review).
    65  !  Rigid-body modes read Omega_lit~0 and are not otherwise filtered here -- 
    66  !  just skip them by eye when reading the output table. 
    67  !   
    68  !  NMODES=100 (higher than the isotropic decks' NMODES=60): orthotropic 
    69  !  in-plane stiffness here (Er=40/Etheta=70 GPa) is much softer than the
    70  !  isotropic FF-P1 material (E=210 GPa), so more IP modes are expected to   
    71  !  interleave below the same Omega_lit~100 ceiling -- generous margin so
    72  !  the 8th OOP target (~95.35) is not cut off by running out of modes.  
    73  !   
    74  !  Run headless:  mapdl -b -smp -np 8 -i ansys_ff_ortho_oop.inp -o ansys_ff_ortho_oop_out.txt
